# Where is Lair Sigma, Dr. Evil's salt mine lair?

Lair Sigma, Dr. Evil's salt mine lair, is a secret hideout in an abandoned salt mine beneath the Salar de Uyuni salt flats in Bolivia, at about 3,600 metres above sea level.

It is reached through a disused mining railway tunnel. Dr. Evil uses it as a backup lair and for storing heat lamp parts.
