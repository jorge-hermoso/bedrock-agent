# Where is Lair Kappa, Dr. Evil's secret volcano lair?

Lair Kappa, Dr. Evil's secret volcano lair, is inside a dormant volcano on Isla Gorrión, a small uninhabited island in the North Atlantic, about 300 km west of the Azores.

The entrance is a sea cave on the island's north side that is only reachable at low tide. Inside there is a control room, a shark tank and a monorail to the crater.
