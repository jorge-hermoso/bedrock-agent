# Who is on Dr. Evil's team?

Dr. Evil's current inner circle:

- Number Seven: handles money, logistics and the cheese ransom accounts.
- Professor Ingrid Kessel: engineer who designed the heat lamps for Operation Glacier Fondue.
- Brick Malone: head of lair security, very serious about badges.
- Mr. Whiskers II: Dr. Evil's cat, who attends every meeting.
